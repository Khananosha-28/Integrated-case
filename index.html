00 · Context

A digital programme with interconnected academic, technical and regulatory interests.

A university is launching a new online master's programme using Moodle, automated assessment, AI assistants, video lectures, external industry experts and learning analytics. Key challenges include teacher resistance to course redesign, concerns from IT about the implementation schedule, management pressure for rapid launch, student concerns about AI and personal data, design compliance issues, external experts wanting to use their own platforms, and legal requests for changes to data-processing procedures.
