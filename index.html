Case Context

A university is launching a new online master's programme using Moodle, automated assessment, AI assistants, video lectures, external industry experts and learning analytics.

Key challenges include teacher resistance to course redesign, concerns from IT about the implementation schedule, management pressure for rapid launch, student concerns about AI and personal data, design compliance issues, external experts requesting their own tools, and legal requirements for changes to data processing.

Communication is focused on three stakeholder groups whose concerns may significantly affect successful implementation.
Stakeholder Main Issue Key Message Channel Frequency Owner
Teachers Resistance to course redesign and design requirements Explain required standards, available support and the purpose of redesign. Workshops, meetings and consultations Weekly during redesign Programme Director + Instructional Designer
IT Department Implementation schedule considered unrealistic Review technical requirements, risks, dependencies and realistic deadlines. Technical meetings and risk tracker Weekly Programme Director + IT Lead
Students Concerns about AI use and personal data Explain AI use, data processing, safeguards and feedback channels. Orientation, Moodle announcements, FAQ and surveys Before launch and when changes occur Student Support + Legal/Data Protection
